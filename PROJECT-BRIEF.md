# Los Mariachis — Project Brief

## What we're building

A small marketing website for Los Mariachis, a local Mexican restaurant client.

**Scope: 4 pages**

1. **Landing page** — elegant, premium feel with a Mexican touch in the design. Hero section already started.
2. **About**
3. **Contact**
4. **Menu** — the page that matters most operationally; the owner needs to edit it themselves.

## Design direction

- Elegant / premium first, "Mexican" as an accent rather than a theme — restrained palette, refined typography, texture and motif used sparingly. Not cantina clip-art.
- Consistent with Jose's usual standard: high-performance, near-perfect Lighthouse scores.

## CMS

**PageCMS** — so the owner can update:

- Menu items
- Prices
- Specials

Content that changes goes in CMS collections; layout and design stay in code.

## Background / why this site exists

- The business currently has **no website**.
- An unaffiliated parked domain, `losmariachisrestaurantonline.net`, ranks #1 for the business name and serves adult content. The owner never bought it; the registrant is unreachable.
- WHOIS: registrar Gname 259 Inc, created Oct 20 2025, expires Oct 20 2026, ParkLogic monetization nameservers.
- That domain is **not** linked in the business's Google Business Profile.
- Printed menu cards show the old domain plus a QR code — so any new domain needs a plan for the printed materials.

**Owner's priorities (ranked):**

1. Get the parked site removed from Google
2. Outrank it with the restaurant's own site
3. Recover the domain

This site is priority #2 — the practical lever Jose controls. SEO fundamentals (title/meta, LocalBusiness + Menu schema, NAP consistency with the Google Business Profile, a clear brand-name H1) matter more than usual here, because outranking the squatter is the point.

## Open items

- Final domain name for the new site (and whether printed cards / QR get reprinted to match)
- Framework confirmation — Astro is the natural fit with PageCMS and matches prior work (OCHO site)
- Menu source: does the owner have the current menu in a usable digital format, or does it need transcribing from the printed cards?
- Photography: real food/interior photos vs. stock
